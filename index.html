How It Began

A home you felt
fresh and open in.

Nuvra began the way most real things do — not with a business plan, but with a feeling.

"Growing up in Pithoragarh, home was never just four walls. It was the mountain air through the windows every morning. The way light hit the hills at 7am and made everything feel like it was beginning again."

When we decided to open our home to travellers, we weren't thinking about occupancy rates. We were thinking about that feeling — and whether a stranger, arriving tired from the road, could feel it too.

They can. They do.

Nuvra Stays is our family's way of sharing what we were lucky enough to grow up with. A home that feels fresh and open. Mountains that don't need a filter. Mornings that make you want to stay one more day.

We started in Pithoragarh. We're growing across the North — Mohali, then deeper into the Himalayas, into Manali, Rishikesh, and beyond. Every listing carries the same intention: that whoever walks through the door leaves feeling more themselves than when they arrived.
