Policy

Last updated · January 2025

Nuvra Stays respects your privacy. This policy explains what personal information we collect, how we use it, and your rights in relation to it.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect the following information when you contact us, make a booking, or use our website:

  • Your name and contact details (phone number, email address)
  • Booking information (dates, number of guests, special requests)
  • Payment information (processed through secure third-party platforms — we do not store card details)
  • Communications you send us via WhatsApp, email, or social media
  • Basic website usage data (pages visited, device type) via cookies or analytics tools

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect solely to:

  • Confirm and manage your reservation
  • Communicate with you about your stay
  • Respond to enquiries and collaboration requests
  • Improve our services and website
  • Send occasional updates about Nuvra Stays (only with your consent)
We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

3. Third-Party Platforms

If you book through Airbnb or another platform, that platform's privacy policy also applies to your data. Nuvra Stays receives only the information the platform shares with hosts as part of the booking process.

4. Data Storage & Security

Your information is stored securely and accessed only by Nuvra Stays team members who need it to manage your booking or enquiry. We take reasonable precautions to protect your data but cannot guarantee absolute security over internet transmissions.

5. Cookies

Our website may use basic cookies to improve your browsing experience. These do not collect personally identifiable information. You can disable cookies in your browser settings at any time.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you
  • Ask us to correct inaccurate data
  • Ask us to delete your data (subject to any legal obligations we may have)
  • Opt out of any marketing communications

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at nuvrastays@gmail.com.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. The current version will always be available on this page. Significant changes will be communicated where possible.

8. Contact

For any privacy-related questions, contact us at nuvrastays@gmail.com or via WhatsApp at +91 98781 03216.
